Games and the software around them.

Games4Fun is a company I built with partners. We work on several games, along with platforms and tools for the game industry.

That brings together game design, software engineering, and product development: the games people play, the services they use to discover them, and the companions that support the experience.

ORBOLTA, 21 Rifts, and more.

ORBOLTA — our first game launch.

ORBOLTA was the first title we launched at Games4Fun. It is part of a growing collection of games we work on as a company.

21 Rifts — another game in development.

21 Rifts is one example of our ongoing game development: a card-building roguelite built in Godot, with iteration and playtesting continuing alongside our other projects.

Platforms and tools for the game industry.

Our work also includes a game deals site and game companions. These products extend Games4Fun’s work into discovery and supporting players around the games they enjoy.

The same engineering interests run through this work: useful interfaces, connected services, and software that supports an experience over time.

A closer look: building 21 Rifts.

In 21 Rifts, players build card combinations, improve a limited deck, and choose routes through a branching campaign. The challenge is making those decisions and their consequences readable.

Separate rules from presentation.

The native Godot implementation keeps game rules separate from the interface. The presentation reveals committed outcomes rather than calculating rewards or advancing randomness itself.

Design for a whole run.

Deck changes, route choices, and saved runs need to work together. Playtesting examines both whether an outcome is correct and whether a player can understand it.

This is one project within Games4Fun’s wider work, rather than the full picture of the company.

Several products, different stages.

ORBOLTA was our first game launch. 21 Rifts remains in development and playtesting, with private source. Our wider portfolio also includes other games, the deals platform, and game companions.
